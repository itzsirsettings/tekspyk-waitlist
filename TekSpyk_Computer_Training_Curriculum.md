# PROFESSIONAL COMPUTER TRAINING SKILLS PROGRAM

## Beginner to Advanced Level

### Program Options

* **3-Month Intensive Program**
* **6-Month Comprehensive Program**
* Training Level: Beginner → Intermediate → Advanced
* Delivery: Theory + Practical + Projects
* Assessment: Weekly exercises, practical tests, projects, and final examination
* Certification: Certificate awarded upon successful completion
