# TekSpyk Waitlist

## Register

brand

TekSpyk is a learning platform with a public multi-step learner registration flow, classes, lessons, videos, and learner progress.

## Users and purpose

Ambitious learners (students, working professionals, founders, career switchers) who want to learn AI-powered software engineering by shipping real products. They arrive from social links or word of mouth, skim the landing page on mobile or desktop, and decide in under a minute whether to join us.

Primary job: help learners choose a path, create an account, and continue into the learning workspace.

## Brand personality

Confident, clean, product-grade. The user chose viewmax.io as the exact visual reference: bright white surfaces, bold near-black display type in an SF Pro voice, one committed blue, generous whitespace, quiz-funnel onboarding with one question per screen. Identity preservation of that reference wins over greenfield reflex-reject rules; the reference lane is deliberate and user-mandated.

## Anti-references

- Dark glassmorphism with cyan glows (the previous design; explicitly replaced)
- Generic SaaS gradient-blob heroes
- Eyebrow kicker labels above section headings (user ban)
- Em-dashes anywhere in copy (user ban)
- Gradient text

## Accessibility

WCAG AA. Keyboard-completable quiz, visible focus rings, 4.5:1 body contrast, reduced-motion alternatives for marquee and step transitions.

## Strategic design principles

1. One idea per fold, long scroll, single blue CTA repeated.
2. Every CTA routes to /onboarding, the quiz funnel, mirroring the reference's Start Creating flow.
3. Copy is short, declarative sentences. No hype filler.
4. Motion clarifies flow (step transitions, marquee, curriculum loop) and never gates content visibility.
